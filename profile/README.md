# AI Works Studio

**Practical AI systems for real work.**

AI Works Studio is an applied AI studio building useful software, automations, and agent-powered workflows. We turn ideas and operational bottlenecks into working tools that people can test, use, and improve.

## What we build

- Custom software for real business workflows
- AI agents with clear goals, controls, and human approval points
- Automations that reduce repetitive work
- Prototypes that validate an idea before heavy investment
- Shared infrastructure that makes AI products easier to build and operate

## How we work

We start with the work itself: the people involved, the decisions they make, and the friction slowing them down. Then we build the smallest useful system, test it in practice, and expand only where the evidence supports it.

Our principles:

- **Useful over impressive.** The result should solve a real problem.
- **Human-directed.** People remain in control of important decisions.
- **Evidence-driven.** Working software and measurable outcomes beat speculation.
- **Responsible by design.** Privacy, security, cost, and failure modes are product requirements.
- **Built together.** The studio is a collaborative home for builders with complementary skills.
